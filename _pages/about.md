About me
======
I received my Ph.D. in computer science in 2004 from the University of Amsterdam ([UvA](https://www.uva.nl/)). I am an associate professor and the chair of the Multiscale Networked Systems ([MNS](https://mns-research.nl/)) research group in the Informatics Institute ([IvI](https://ivi.uva.nl/)) at UvA. I am the technical manager of the Virtual Lab and Innovation Center ([VLIC](https://www.lifewatch.eu/organisation-governance/vlab-innovation-centre/)) of [LifeWatch ERIC](https://www.lifewatch.eu/), a European research infrastructure in ecology and biodiversity science.

My research addresses software challenges in developing and operating quality‑critical applications across distributed infrastructures. We research and develop innovative performance‑optimisation and data‑management techniques to achieve robust, trustworthy quality‑critical services in Cloud and Edge computing environments.

The work is primarily funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) and EU Horizon programmes through multiple projects. I am the Co-PI of the Dutch [LTER-LIFE](https://lter-life.nl/en) project and coordinate the UvA effort in the EU projects [ENVRI-Hub NEXT](https://envri.eu/envri-community-contributes-to-envri-hub-next-advancing-interdisciplinary-environmental-research-data-access/), [EVERSE](https://everse.software/), [OSCARS](https://oscars-project.eu/) and [Blue-Cloud 2026](https://blue-cloud.org/) to develop Digital Twin Virtual Research Environments, a research assets search engine, and Cloud automation and optimisation solutions.

I coordinated the project [SWITCH](http://www.switchproject.eu/) (Software Workbench for Interactive, Time-Critical and Highly Self-Adaptive Cloud Applications). I led the *Data for Science* theme in the environmental science cluster project [ENVRIplus](http://www.envriplus.eu/), and the technical development work packages in the [ENVRI-FAIR](http://envri.eu/envri-fair/), [ARTICONF](http://articonf.eu/) and [CLARIFY](http://www.clarify-project.eu/) projects. I am an IEEE Senior Member and the managing editor of the [Journal of Cloud Computing](https://journalofcloudcomputing.springeropen.com/).
